## 1. Arctic Glass

**A minimal polar aesthetic where translucent panels float over a soft gradient sky.**

- **Colors**: `#E8F4FD`, `#B3D9F7`, `#FFFFFF` with `rgba` glass panels
- **Typography**: Inter or SF Pro, light weight (300)
- **Cards**: Frosted glass (`backdrop-filter: blur`), thin white borders, subtle shadow
- **Density**: Airy — generous padding, wide gutters

## 10. Weatherglass
